About

I'm a Senior Software Engineer at Wells Fargo, working at the intersection of GenAI and high-throughput backend systems for fixed-income trading. My work spans agentic LLM pipelines (LangGraph orchestrator + validator with MCP tool-use and governance), neural retrieval, and distributed PnL microservices hardened for replay-safety, exactly-once Kafka semantics, and idempotent ingestion.

I joined Wells Fargo as an intern in 2021, came back full-time in 2022 right after graduating from NIT Calicut, and was promoted to Senior in 2026. Along the way I picked up a Top Achievers Award, an Innovation Pitch Day finalist spot, and an IEEE-published paper on metaheuristic feature selection.

Outside of work I tinker — PyQt6 desktop AI, local LLM tooling, GAN experiments, and the occasional weekend hack. The portfolio below has a slice.
